Good to know

Questions before you go?

Are these live, bookable farms?

We are currently onboarding and verifying farm partners across Telangana and Karnataka. Submitting an enquiry expresses your early interest and reserves your spot on our launch invite list.

What should I wear?

Comfortable cotton clothing, closed walking shoes or boots, a broad-brim hat, and sunscreen are ideal. We recommend carrying a refillable water bottle.

Can young children and seniors participate?

Yes! Many experiences are gentle farm walks and shaded craft workshops tailored specifically for multi-generational families. Each listing indicates accessibility notes.

How do bookings work?

Submit your preferred date and group size. Our concierge matches your group with available farm hosts and sends confirmation details. No online payment is taken on this MVP site.

How do I list my farm?

Click "Become a farm partner". Our rural partnership team visits your property to understand activities, amenities, safety standards, and visitor arrangements.
