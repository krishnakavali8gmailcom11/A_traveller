Privacy Notice

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This is a starter privacy notice for the launch-stage website. Have it reviewed and replace contact details before public launch.

Information collected

We collect details submitted in enquiry forms, such as name, phone number, email, city, preferred date, guest count, farm details and enquiry notes.

Purpose

We use this information to respond to enquiries, understand demand in launch cities, evaluate farm partnerships and coordinate requested experiences. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking.

Storage and sharing

Enquiries are stored in the website database and accessed by authorised project administrators. We may use service providers required to host and operate the website.

Retention and requests

Retain information only as long as needed for the stated purposes and applicable obligations. For access, correction, deletion or withdrawal of consent, contact privacy@onedayfarmer.in. Verify this address before launch.

Security

The starter project includes validation, rate limiting and restricted admin access. Production launch still requires HTTPS, secure hosting, backups, secret management, monitoring and a reviewed privacy process. Do not submit sensitive personal information.
