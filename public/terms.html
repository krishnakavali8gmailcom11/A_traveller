Enquiries only

Submitting an enquiry or joining a city interest list does not create a booking, reserve a place or require payment. A booking exists only after the operator confirms all details in writing through an authorised channel.

Experience details

Activities, facilities, timings, accessibility, food, age suitability and seasonal availability vary by host and must be confirmed before travel.

Farm partners

Partner applications are subject to verification, written agreement, applicable permissions, safety review and operational readiness. Submission does not guarantee listing or partnership.

Changes

These terms are a draft and may be updated before launch. Add full legal entity, address, governing law, cancellation/refund, liability and dispute terms after professional review.
